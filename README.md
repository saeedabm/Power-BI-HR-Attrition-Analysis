# HR Employee Attrition Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard explores workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, work-life balance, overtime, job roles, and other factors associated with employee turnover.

The objective is to identify attrition patterns and provide meaningful insights that can support HR teams in understanding employee retention challenges.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Understand the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.
- Analyze attrition across age groups and employee demographics.
- Explore the impact of job satisfaction, engagement, and work-life balance.
- Examine employee perceptions of AI-related job risk.
- Provide actionable insights to support employee retention.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation

## Key Analysis Areas
- Employee demographics
- Department-wise and job-role-wise attrition
- Age and tenure analysis
- Overtime and work-life balance
- Job satisfaction and employee engagement
- Perceived AI-related job risk

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive filters and slicers
- Employee and attrition KPI cards
- Department-wise attrition analysis
- Top job roles by attrition
- Employee demographic analysis
- Work-life balance and overtime analysis
- Employee engagement and satisfaction analysis
- AI-related job risk analysis
- Business insights and visual storytelling

## Project Outcome
This project demonstrates the use of Power BI to explore HR data, identify employee attrition patterns, and communicate workforce insights through interactive dashboards.
